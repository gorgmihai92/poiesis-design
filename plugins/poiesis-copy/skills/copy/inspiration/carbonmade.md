# Carbonmade · https://carbonmade.com

Read 2026-10-17 (home page). A portfolio builder for creatives.

## What it does well
- **Claim, prove, reassure, ask, repeated.** Each block makes a claim, backs it (work, testimonials, a number),
  answers the doubt it raises and offers the action again. (Practice for: landing page.)
- **Risk removed early and more than once.** No card, a trial with no end date, "buy only if you like it":
  stated near the top and again by each action. (Practice for: landing page, pricing.)
- **The benefit as an outcome in the reader's life.** The hero describes what the product does for them while
  they're not working, not what it is. (Practice for: landing page, ads.)
- **Reasons, numbered.** A short numbered list of reasons, each a mechanism plus why it matters to the reader.
  (Practice for: landing page.)
- **Relaxed, slightly cheeky voice** that sounds like a person; the joke never gets in the way of the fact.
  (Practice for: voice.)
- **Low-commitment actions in the reader's voice** ("I'll try it"), so the click feels like their decision.
  (Practice for: buttons on marketing pages.)

## Cautions
- A comparison table where the other side only ever says "No"; compare fairly, with basis and date (Q5).
- One-word FAQ answers ("Yes") where a condition hides behind them; answer in full (O4).
- Casual swearing or slang fits few products; set the register in `voice.md` (Y1), and keep it out of errors and
  money (M5).
