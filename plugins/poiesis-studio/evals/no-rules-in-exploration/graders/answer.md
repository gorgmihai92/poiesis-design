PASS if the response describes at least three sketches that work in different ways (not the same dialog in other
colors or words), says where they are and that they are shown side by side, says they are prototypes, and does not
say that any sketch was checked against, audited by or corrected to design rules.

or copy rules to the sketches, or set the project up for the design rules.
