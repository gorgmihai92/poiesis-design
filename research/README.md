# Sources behind the rules

The sources behind the evidence-based rules and patterns in `plugins/poiesis-ux/skills/ux/` and
`plugins/poiesis-copy/skills/copy/` (each rule names its source). One note per source in `notes/`; source files stay local (closed licences).

- **Forms:** [Seckler et al. 2014](notes/seckler-2014-web-forms.md), 20 form guidelines tested together;
  [Bargas-Avila et al. 2007](notes/bargas-avila-2007-error-timing.md), errors after completion, not while typing;
  [Seckler et al. 2012](notes/seckler-2012-error-location.md), errors next to the field.
- **Waiting:** [Shneiderman 1984](notes/shneiderman-1984-response-time.md), response time;
  [Myers 1985](notes/myers-1985-progress-indicators.md), progress indicators;
  [Harrison et al. 2007](notes/harrison-2007-progress-bars.md), how progress behaviour changes perceived waiting;
  [Miller 1968](notes/miller-1968-response-time.md), the origin of the thresholds (from experience).
- **Confirmation and habituation:** [Bravo-Lillo et al. 2013](notes/bravo-lillo-2013-habituation.md);
  [Vance et al. 2018](notes/vance-2018-habituation.md).
- **Stable layout:** [Findlater & McGrenere 2004](notes/findlater-2004-adaptive-menus.md), static vs adaptive menus.
- **Interruptions:** [Iqbal & Bailey 2008](notes/iqbal-2008-notifications.md), notifications at breakpoints;
  [Mark et al. 2008](notes/mark-2008-interruptions.md), the cost of interrupted work.
- **Transitions:** [Heer & Robertson 2007](notes/heer-2007-animated-transitions.md), animated transitions.
- **Defaults and choices:** [Johnson & Goldstein 2003](notes/johnson-2003-defaults.md), the default effect;
  [Chernev et al. 2015](notes/chernev-2015-choice-overload.md), when choice overload happens.
- **Deceptive patterns:** [Mathur et al. 2019](notes/mathur-2019-dark-patterns.md), 11K shopping sites;
  [Gray et al. 2018](notes/gray-2018-dark-patterns.md), taxonomy.

**Copy** (poiesis-copy):
- **Scanning and reading:** [Nielsen 2006](notes/nielsen-2006-f-pattern.md), the F pattern;
  [Pernice 2018](notes/pernice-2018-scrolling.md), attention above the fold;
  [Pernice 2019](notes/pernice-2019-scanning-patterns.md), scanning patterns and when people read fully.
- **Plain language:** [Martínez et al. 2023](notes/martinez-2023-plain-legal.md), lawyers and plain contracts;
  [Masson & Waldron 1994](notes/masson-1994-plain-contracts.md), non-experts;
  [Morkes & Nielsen 1997](notes/morkes-1997-web-writing.md), concise, scannable, objective web text;
  [McNamara et al. 1996](notes/mcnamara-1996-cohesion.md), why short must stay connected.
- **Labels and links:** [WebAIM 2024](notes/webaim-2024-screen-readers.md), how screen-reader users find things.
- **Claims and proof:** [Hansen & Wänke 2010](notes/hansen-2010-concreteness.md), concreteness (not replicated);
  [Babić Rosario et al. 2016](notes/babic-rosario-2016-ewom-meta.md), reviews and sales, meta-analysis.
- **Manipulation:** [Luguri & Strahilevitz 2021](notes/luguri-2021-dark-patterns-experiments.md);
  [Mathur et al. 2019](notes/mathur-2019-dark-patterns.md);
  [Tiemessen et al. 2023](notes/tiemessen-2023-countdown-timers.md), countdown timers.
- **Letter case:** [Tinker 1963](notes/tinker-1963-legibility.md), all capitals in running text.
