# The Effect of Electronic Word of Mouth on Sales (Babić Rosario, Sotgiu, De Valck & Bijmolt, 2016)

- **Source:** Journal of Marketing Research 53(3): 297–318 · https://doi.org/10.1509/jmr.14.0380
- **File:** none
- **Licence:** open; not stored.
- **Evidence:** meta-analysis · 96 studies, 1,532 effect sizes · **abstract read**.

## What it says
- Online word of mouth (reviews, ratings) is positively linked to sales; the average correlation is small (.091)
  and varies by platform and product. Volume mattered more than valence.

## What it means for the plugin
- Real proof helps, modestly (Q2). Fake reviews also work and readers rarely spot them, so the rule is about
  honesty and law (EU 2019/2161), not about backfire.
