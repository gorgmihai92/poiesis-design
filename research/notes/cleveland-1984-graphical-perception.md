## What it says (from the abstract)
- People read a graph by carrying out elementary perceptual tasks, and these can be ordered by how accurately
  people perform them.
- The experiments support parts of the ordering and suggest the set of tasks should be larger.
- The guideline drawn from it: a graph should use tasks as high in the ordering as possible.
- Applied to bar charts, divided bar charts, pie charts and shaded statistical maps, the authors offer dot charts,
  dot charts with grouping and framed-rectangle charts in their place.
