# Predictability and accuracy in adaptive user interfaces (Gajos, Everitt, Tan, Czerwinski & Weld, 2008)

- **Source:** Proceedings of CHI 2008, pp. 1271–1274 · https://doi.org/10.1145/1357054.1357252
- **File:** none (abstract only)
- **Licence:** © ACM: closed.
- **Evidence:** empirical · **abstract only**.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- More predictable and more accurate adaptation both improved satisfaction strongly; accuracy improved performance
  and use of the adaptive interface more than predictability did.

## What it means for the plugin
- Adaptation is not harmful in itself; inaccurate, unpredictable adaptation is. Suggestions belong in a separate,
  labelled place rather than reordering what people already know.
