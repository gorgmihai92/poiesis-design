# The dark (patterns) side of UX design (Gray, Kou, Battles, Hoggatt & Toombs, 2018)

- **Source:** Proceedings of CHI 2018, Paper 534 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3173574.3174108
- **File:** none (abstract only)
- **Licence:** © ACM: closed.
- **Evidence:** analytical (content analysis of 118 practitioner-identified examples) · **abstract and summaries only**.

## What it says
- Five strategies: nagging (repeated intrusions unrelated to the task), obstruction (making an action harder than
  it needs to be, to dissuade it), sneaking (hiding, disguising or delaying information), interface interference,
  forced action.

## What it means for the plugin
- The categories behind the "no dark patterns" rule.
