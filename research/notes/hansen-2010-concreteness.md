# Truth from Language and Truth from Fit (Hansen & Wänke, 2010)

- **Source:** Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin 36(11): 1576–1588 · https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167210386238
- **File:** none
- **Licence:** closed; not stored.
- **Evidence:** empirical · **abstract-level; a preregistered replication (Henderson, Vallée-Tourangeau & Simons) found smaller, inconsistent effects**.

## What it says
- Statements in more concrete language were judged more likely true. The effect did not replicate cleanly.
- No meta-analysis on concreteness, precision and credibility was found; a large test of precise numbers
  (n = 1,505, public-sector performance) found no convincing effect.

## What it means for the plugin
- Specific, checkable claims are required for honesty (Q1), not because they are proven to persuade more.
