# Animated transitions in statistical data graphics (Heer & Robertson, 2007)

- **Source:** IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics 13(6): 1240–1247 · https://doi.org/10.1109/TVCG.2007.70539 (pages = PDF pages)
- **File:** local only (author copy)
- **Licence:** © IEEE: local only.
- **Evidence:** empirical · two controlled experiments, 24 participants (p. 8).

## What it says
- Animated transitions between related statistical charts improved people's perception of what changed, compared
  with an instant switch (p. 1, 8). Staging helped in some cases but raised tracking error in scatter plots, and
  heavy staging raised error in stacked bars and donuts (p. 7); about 1 s per stage worked (p. 4, 7).

## What it means for the plugin
- When a data display changes in place after a person's action, a simple transition (one or two stages) shows
  where items went; within the motion rule I5. The study covers charts, not every kind of view.
