## What it says (from the abstract)
- Design research can produce knowledge more abstract than a single design and less general than a theory.
- The authors name one such form a strong concept. It carries a core design idea that can generate new designs
  across situations of use and even domains; it concerns interactive behavior, not static appearance; it is a part
  of an artifact and also speaks of how people use it over time; it sits above particular instances.
- Two examples are given, social navigation and seamfulness, and the authors discuss how such a concept can be
  contestable, defensible and substantive.
