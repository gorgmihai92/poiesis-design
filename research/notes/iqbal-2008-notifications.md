# Effects of intelligent notification management on users and their tasks (Iqbal & Bailey, 2008)

- **Source:** Proceedings of CHI 2008, pp. 93–102 · https://doi.org/10.1145/1357054.1357070 (pages = PDF pages)
- **File:** local only (author copy)
- **Licence:** © ACM: local only.
- **Evidence:** empirical · two studies; the second, on effects, had 16 participants (p. 6).

## What it says
- Delivering notifications at breakpoints in the task reduced frustration compared with immediate delivery in the
  diagram-editing task at medium breakpoints, not significantly in programming; the reaction-time gain was marginal
  (p < 0.056) after grouping breakpoint types (p. 9). The authors limit it to content-creation tasks (p. 9).

## What it means for the plugin
- Non-urgent notifications wait for a natural pause (after a save, between steps), not in the middle of typing.
