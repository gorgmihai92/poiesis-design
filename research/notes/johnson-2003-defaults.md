# Do defaults save lives? (Johnson & Goldstein, 2003)

- **Source:** Science 302(5649): 1338–1339 · https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1091721
- **File:** none (abstract and summaries only)
- **Licence:** © AAAS: closed.
- **Evidence:** empirical (online experiment, 161 participants, plus country comparison) · **secondary summaries only**;
  a later replication found the effect smaller.

## What it says
- Most people keep the pre-selected option: organ-donor consent was far higher where it was the default.

## What it means for the plugin
- Defaults decide for most people, so a default is a design decision with consequences: pick the option that
  serves the person, and never pre-select a cost, a subscription or data sharing.
