# Shining a Light on Dark Patterns (Luguri & Strahilevitz, 2021)

- **Source:** Journal of Legal Analysis 13(1): 43–109 · https://doi.org/10.1093/jla/laaa006
- **File:** none (abstract only)
- **Licence:** open access (Oxford University Press); not stored.
- **Evidence:** empirical · two experiments with representative U.S. samples · **abstract only**.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- Mild and aggressive dark patterns substantially increased sign-ups for a dubious service; aggressive ones caused
  more backlash; less-educated participants were more susceptible.
- Hidden information, trick questions and obstruction were especially effective; countdown timers and
  "recommended" labels were not, in this experiment.

## What it means for the plugin
- Deceptive patterns change decisions measurably, most of all hidden information, trick questions and obstruction.
