# The cost of interrupted work: more speed and stress (Mark, Gudith & Klocke, 2008)

- **Source:** Proceedings of CHI 2008, pp. 107–110 · https://doi.org/10.1145/1357054.1357072 (pages = PDF pages)
- **File:** local only (author copy)
- **Licence:** © ACM: local only.
- **Evidence:** empirical · lab experiment (p. 1).

## What it says
- Interrupted people finished as fast and as well, by working faster, but with more stress, frustration, time
  pressure and effort; whether the interruption matched the task's topic made no difference (p. 1, 4).

## What it means for the plugin
- Interruptions cost even when quality looks unchanged; keep them few and deferrable.
