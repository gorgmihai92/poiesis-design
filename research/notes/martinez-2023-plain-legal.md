# Even Lawyers Do Not Like Legalese (Martínez, Mollica & Gibson, 2023)

- **Source:** PNAS 120(23) · https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2302672120
- **File:** none
- **Licence:** PNAS; not stored.
- **Evidence:** empirical · two preregistered experiments, 105 US lawyers · **read via a press write-up; effect sizes not seen**.

## What it says
- Lawyers understood and recalled contracts in plain English better than in legalese, and rated the plain
  versions as higher quality and no less enforceable.

## What it means for the plugin
- Plain words help experts too (L3). Legal contracts only; no web or UI copy tested.
