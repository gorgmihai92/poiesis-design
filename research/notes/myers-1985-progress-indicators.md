# The importance of percent-done progress indicators for computer-human interfaces (Myers, 1985)

- **Source:** Proceedings of CHI '85, pp. 11–17 · https://doi.org/10.1145/317456.317459 (pages = PDF pages)
- **File:** local only (author copy)
- **Licence:** © ACM: local only.
- **Evidence:** empirical · formal experiment with a database query system, 48 participants, mostly computer
  science students (p. 5).

## What it says
- People prefer systems with progress indicators; indicators show the command was accepted and let experienced
  users estimate completion and plan their time (p. 1, 7).
- No significant preference for constant over variable response time was found (p. 1).
