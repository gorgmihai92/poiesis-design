# F-Shaped Pattern for Reading Web Content (Nielsen, 2006)

- **Source:** Nielsen Norman Group article, 2006 · https://www.nngroup.com/articles/f-shaped-pattern-reading-web-content-discovered/
- **File:** none
- **Licence:** web article; not stored.
- **Evidence:** practitioner eye-tracking · 232 users, thousands of pages · **article read via a research summary**.

## What it says
- On text-heavy pages people often scan in a rough F: across the top, a shorter second sweep, then down the left
  edge. Variants (E, inverted L) occur; it is a tendency, not a law.
- Not independently replicated in the sources found; task and layout change it.

## What it means for the plugin
- The first words of headings and lines carry the most weight (L2, O1). Not cited as a universal law.
