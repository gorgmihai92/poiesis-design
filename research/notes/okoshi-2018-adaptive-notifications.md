# Real-world large-scale study on adaptive notification scheduling on smartphones (Okoshi, Tsubouchi & Tokuda, 2018)

- **Source:** Pervasive and Mobile Computing 50: 1–24 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pmcj.2018.07.005
- **File:** none (abstract only)
- **Licence:** © Elsevier: closed.
- **Evidence:** empirical · field study in a major Android app, more than 680,000 users over three weeks · **abstract only**.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- Delaying notifications until an interruptible moment was detected cut user response time by 49.7% compared with
  immediate delivery, with more notifications opened and steady engagement.

## What it means for the plugin
- Field support, on mobile, for holding non-urgent notifications until a natural break. Outcomes are response and
  engagement, not wellbeing.
