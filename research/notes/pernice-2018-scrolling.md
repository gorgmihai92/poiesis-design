# Scrolling and Attention (Pernice, 2018)

- **Source:** Nielsen Norman Group article, 2018 · https://www.nngroup.com/articles/scrolling-and-attention/
- **File:** none
- **Licence:** web article; not stored.
- **Evidence:** practitioner eye-tracking · 120 participants, over 130,000 fixations · **article read via a research summary**.

## What it says
- 57% of viewing time was above the fold (80% in the 2010 study); 74% in the first two screenfuls, 81% in the
  first three.
- Page length was not controlled. A vendor log study (Chartbeat) finds most engaged time below the fold among
  readers who scroll: the measures differ, the first screen still decides whether people scroll.
