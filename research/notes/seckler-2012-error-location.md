# User-friendly locations of error messages in web forms: Put them on the right side of the erroneous input field (Seckler, Tuch, Opwis & Bargas-Avila, 2012)

- **Source:** Interacting with Computers 24(3): 107–118 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.intcom.2012.03.002
- **File:** none (the repository refused automated download)
- **Licence:** © Elsevier: closed.
- **Evidence:** empirical · six error-message locations, 303 participants · **abstract only**.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- Messages next to the erroneous field gave significantly better performance than messages at the top or bottom of
  the form; the right side of the field worked best.

## What it means for the plugin
- Each error sits next to its field (beside it where the layout allows), not only in a summary at the top.
