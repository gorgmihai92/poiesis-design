# Response time and display rate in human performance with computers (Shneiderman, 1984)

- **Source:** ACM Computing Surveys 16(3): 265–285 · https://doi.org/10.1145/2514.2517 (pages = PDF pages)
- **File:** local only (author copy)
- **Licence:** © ACM: local only.
- **Evidence:** review of experiments.

## What it says
- Frequent users prefer response times under a second for most tasks, and productivity rises as response time
  falls (p. 1).

## What it means for the plugin
- Supports "every action answers within about 1 s" (F1). The often-quoted 0.1 / 1 / 10 s thresholds go back to
  Miller (1968), whose figures came from experience rather than experiments; treat them as conventions.
