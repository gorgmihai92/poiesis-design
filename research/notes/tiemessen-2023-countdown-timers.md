# The Time is Ticking: Limited Time Discounts (Tiemessen et al., 2023)

- **Source:** CHI EA '23 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3544549.3585735
- **File:** none
- **Licence:** ACM; not stored.
- **Evidence:** exploratory online experiment · N = 245 · **abstract only**.

## What it says
- Deceptive countdown timers were seen as manipulative and unethical and made people averse to the offer and
  the site. In a related study (Tuncer et al. 2023, N = 202), time and quantity cues lowered perceived vendor
  benevolence.

## What it means for the plugin
- No fake urgency (Q3); with Luguri & Strahilevitz 2021, aggressive manipulation works and also angers people.
