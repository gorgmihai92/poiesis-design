# Legibility of Print (Tinker, 1963)

- **Source:** Iowa State University Press, 1963 (reports Tinker & Paterson's experiments)
- **File:** none
- **Licence:** closed; not stored.
- **Evidence:** empirical · 127 college readers, timed reading of prose · **read via a secondary summary; pages unchecked**.

## What it says
- Running text in all capitals was read about 10–14% slower than lowercase. A later study of single words
  (Kinney & Showman 1967) found capitals as legible or more, for isolated words.
- No study compares sentence case with Title Case; style guides prefer sentence case for consistency.

## What it means for the plugin
- No ALL CAPS for more than a few words; sentence case is a convention (Y4), not a speed claim.
