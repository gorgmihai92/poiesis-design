# Tuning out security warnings: a longitudinal examination of habituation through fMRI, eye tracking, and field experiments (Vance, Jenkins, Anderson, Bjornn & Kirwan, 2018)

- **Source:** MIS Quarterly 42(2): 355–380 · https://doi.org/10.25300/MISQ/2018/14124
- **File:** none (abstract only)
- **Licence:** © MISQ: closed.
- **Evidence:** empirical · fMRI, eye tracking and a three-week field experiment · **abstract only**.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- Attention to repeated warnings declines measurably with exposure; warnings whose appearance varies
  (polymorphic) slowed the decline and kept adherence higher in the field.

## What it means for the plugin
- Repeating the same confirmation trains people to dismiss it; reserve confirmations for what is rare and serious.
