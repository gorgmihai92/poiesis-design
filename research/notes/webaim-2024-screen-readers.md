# Screen Reader User Survey #10 (WebAIM, 2024)

- **Source:** WebAIM · https://webaim.org/projects/screenreadersurvey10/
- **File:** none
- **Licence:** web page; not stored.
- **Evidence:** survey · about 1,500 respondents on the items used · **page read via a research summary**.

## What it says
- 71.6% use headings first to find information on a long page; 4.8% use the list of links first.
- Links or buttons that don't make sense are among the most problematic items reported.

## What it means for the plugin
- Link and button text must make sense alone (B1, B2); headings carry the page (O1). No controlled study of
  "click here" vs descriptive links was found; the rule rests on WCAG 2.4.4 and this survey.
